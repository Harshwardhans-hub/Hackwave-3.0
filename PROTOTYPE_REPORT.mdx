## 1. Problem Statement Deconstruction

### 1.1 The Core Operating Bottleneck
Indian Railways operates over **68,000+ route kilometers**, running **13,000+ passenger trains** and **8,000+ freight rakes** daily. Maintaining track geometry, 25 kV overhead catenary (OHE), and signaling requires **Track Maintenance Blocks (Possessions)** where train movements are suspended.

Currently, maintenance planning across three primary departments operates in silos:
1. **Permanent Way (P-Way / Civil Engineering)** via **TMS** (Track Management System).
2. **Signal & Telecommunication (S&T)** via **SMMS** (Signaling Maintenance Management System).
3. **Traction Distribution (TRD / Electrical)** via **TDMS** (Traction Distribution Management System).

**The Practical Impact:**  
Each department requests separate track blocks for the same corridor segment on different days (e.g., P-Way on Monday, S&T on Tuesday, TRD on Wednesday). This leads to **repeated track closures (7.5+ hours of lost throughput across three days)**, when all three maintenance activities could be bundled into a **single 3.5–4 hour shadow block**. Furthermore, coordination relies on manual paper memos, unintegrated BDMS requests, and phone calls between Section Controllers and Station Masters.

### 1.2 The Four Mandatory PS Requirements
According to the operational mandate, any production solution must address:

| # | Mandate | Practical Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **REQ-1** | **Multi-System Ingestion** | Ingest maintenance demands (defects, overdue tasks) from **TMS**, **SMMS**, and **TDMS**, combined with timetable & occupancy data from **COA** and a **goods trains forecast**. |
| **REQ-2** | **AI/ML Task Prioritization** | Score and rank competing maintenance work orders based on **criticality**, **urgency**, and **network impact**. |
| **REQ-3** | **Constraint Optimization & Bundling** | Optimize block schedules to maximize track uptime by dynamically bundling multi-department requests into synchronized **shadow blocks**. |
| **REQ-4** | **Multi-Horizon Planning** | Output dynamic, multi-tier schedules covering both **7-day tactical** and **30-day strategic** horizons. |

## 2. Competitive Landscape & Our Winning Edge (The 4 USPs)

An in-depth analysis of alternative railway scheduling tools reveals that standard optimization (Google OR-Tools CP-SAT) and basic Gantt charts are **table stakes**—almost every tool attempts them.

To win, Sahayak Rail focuses on the **four critical operational realities that competitors miss**:

```
                                  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
                                  │            SAHAYAK RAIL 4 KEY WINNING USPs              │
                                  └───────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────┘
                                                              │
               ┌──────────────────────────────┬───────────────┴──────────────┬─────────────────────────────┐
               ▼                              ▼                              ▼                             ▼
       ┌───────────────┐              ┌───────────────┐              ┌───────────────┐             ┌───────────────┐
       │    USP #1     │              │    USP #2     │              │    USP #3     │             │    USP #4     │
       │Low-Bandwidth  │              │Freight Flow & │              │Weather / IMD  │             │Cross-Dept Bid │

    • WhatsApp fallback            • "Calm Window" finder         • Rain/heat/scour alerts      • Automated bundling
    • Official Form T/348M         • Preserves freight revenue    • Dynamic rescheduling        • Conflict prevention
```

1. **USP #1 — Low-Connectivity Field Dispatch (Highest Impact):** Track gangmen, PWIs, and S&T supervisors frequently operate in remote sections with zero 4G/5G signal. Sahayak Rail provides a two-way GSM 2G SMS engine for block requests, clearance confirmations, and cryptographic **Private Number (PN)** exchanges, backed by official **Form T/348M** generation.
2. **USP #2 — Goods Train / Freight Forecasting (PS-Mandated):** Passenger trains follow fixed timetables, but freight is demand-driven. Competitors ignore freight; Sahayak Rail models seasonal goods traffic (coal, container, petroleum) to identify real "Calm Maintenance Windows."
3. **USP #3 — Weather & Monsoon Resilience:** Automatically adjusts outdoor track maintenance around high-risk environmental conditions (heavy monsoon washouts, bridge scour, rail temperature > 55°C causing rail expansion).
4. **USP #4 — Cross-Department Bidding Mechanism:** Allows engineering, signaling, and electrical wings to submit maintenance bids for target sections, which the solver clusters into shared possessions.

## 3. Current State of the Frontend Prototype

The existing frontend is an aesthetically polished React 19 application configured with Vite. It features a complete design system adhering to official Indian Railways, CRIS, and IRCTC themes.

### 3.1 Routing & Navigation Architecture
- **Router:** Custom client-side hash routing (`window.location.hash`) inside `src/App.jsx`.
- **Supported Hash Routes:**
  - `/#overview`: Official landing portal with Hero, interactive map, USP showcase, and login trigger.
  - `/#dashboard`: Section Controller command center with 24-hour Gantt chart.
  - `/#optimizer`: CP-SAT solver studio, weight adjusters, and bidding interface.
  - `/#freight`: Freight density charts and calm window detector.
  - `/#field-dispatch`: 2G SMS terminal, syntax parsing, and Form T/348M preview.
  - `/#weather`: IMD weather radar and vulnerable section alerts.
  - `/#audit`: Explainable AI (SHAP) feature attribution and regulatory audit trail.

---

### 3.2 Page-by-Page Technical Audit

#### Page 1: `DashboardPage.jsx` (Section Controller Command Deck)
- **UI Elements:**
  - Division header (`NORTHERN RAILWAY • DELHI DIVISION`).
  - Horizon toggles (`24-Hour Real-Time`, `7-Day Tactical`, `30-Day Strategic`).
  - Emergency toggle button (`Simulate Rail Fracture`).
  - 4 KPI cards: Corridor Availability (94.8%), Track-Hours Saved (4.5 hrs), Clashes Averted (14), Solver Status.
  - 4 Telemetry pills: TMS, SMMS, TDMS, COA.
  - **Interactive 24-Hour Multi-Track Gantt Chart:** Plots blocks on `UP_MAIN`, `DN_MAIN`, and `GOODS_LOOP`.
  - Block Inspector Drawer displaying block details, Private Numbers, and Caution Orders.
- **Current Data Implementation:**
  - **100% Mock Data:** The 19 corridor blocks, 7-day tactical cards, and 30-day strategic items are static JavaScript objects.
  - The "Corridor Section" selector (`NDLS-AGC`, `GZB-ALJN`, etc.) does not fetch new tracks.
  - "Simulate Rail Fracture" simply updates local React state without running a real rescheduling algorithm.
- **Backend Requirement:** Needs REST endpoints for corridor timelines, real train runs, active maintenance possessions, and an emergency re-solve trigger.

#### Page 2: `OptimizerPage.jsx` (CP-SAT Solver & Cross-Dept Bidding)
- **UI Elements:**
  - "Run OR-Tools Optimizer" button with simulated solver progress bar.
  - Siloed vs. Bundled comparison widget (9.0 hrs siloed vs. 3.5 hrs bundled).
  - Multi-objective weight sliders (Punctuality, Safety, Utilization).
  - Department defect ingestion table (TMS, SMMS, TDMS).
  - **Cross-Department Bidding Panel (USP #4):** Table of active bids with an interactive submission form ("Submit Bid to AI Pool").
  - Human-in-the-Loop action buttons: "Approve & Issue PN", "Adjust Window", "Decline & Re-Solve".
- **Current Data Implementation:**
  - **Simulated Solver:** Clicking "Run Optimizer" triggers a 1200ms `setTimeout()` and renders a hardcoded result.
  - **Slider Disconnect:** Changing the weight sliders updates state numbers but has no effect on solver outputs.
  - **In-Memory Bids:** Submitted bids are appended to a temporary React state array and lost on page refresh.
- **Backend Requirement:** Real Python microservice running Google OR-Tools CP-SAT, receiving task inputs and weights, and returning mathematical schedules.

#### Page 3: `FreightForecastPage.jsx` (Goods-Train Density & Calm Windows)

#### Page 4: `FieldDispatchPage.jsx` (2G SMS Terminal & Form T/348M)

## 6. Prioritized Backend Implementation Plan

To balance hackathon deadlines with maximum judge impact, the development sequence is structured into three clear priority tiers:

### Phase 1: High Priority (Core Engine & Table Stakes) — *Do First*
*These deliver an end-to-end working system from data ingestion to optimization and visualization.*

1. **Backend Foundation & Database Setup:**
   - Initialize FastAPI project with CORS middleware.
   - Seed realistic railway data: Corridors (`NDLS-AGC`, `GZB-ALJN`), scheduled trains (Gatimaan, Rajdhani, Vande Bharat, goods rakes), and pending maintenance requests (P-Way, S&T, TRD).
2. **Real Google OR-Tools CP-SAT Solver Service:**
   - Model the block scheduling problem:
     - **Decision Variables:** Block start time, duration, assigned track.
     - **Hard Constraints:** No overlap between trains and maintenance on the same line; minimum safe headway (15 min); OHE isolation safety rules.
     - **Soft Objectives:** Minimize passenger train delay penalty; maximize bundled shadow blocks; satisfy urgent maintenance first.
   - Return realistic execution logs, solve times, and computed metrics.
3. **Task Prioritization Engine:**
   - Formula-based scoring (0–100) combining IRPWM criteria (TGI < 55), overdue days, and line speed potential.
4. **Corridor Gantt API (`/api/corridors/{id}/timeline`):**
   - Provide dynamic 24-hour timeline data so the Gantt chart reflects true solver schedules.
5. **Frontend API Integration:**
   - Replace static data in `DashboardPage.jsx` and `OptimizerPage.jsx` with real API client calls.

---

### Phase 2: Medium Priority (Key Winning USPs) — *Do Next*
*These cover the specific features that separate our prototype from all competitors.*

6. **2G SMS Parser & Dispatch Engine (USP #1):**
   - Implement regex/syntax parser for SMS format:  
     `BLOCK REQ | SEC:<FROM>-<TO> | KM:<START>-<END> | DEPT:<DEPT> | TYPE:<TYPE> | DUR:<HRS>`
   - Validate requested block against current timetable.
   - Auto-generate cryptographic Private Number (`PN-XXXXXX-DIV`).
   - Endpoint to populate and return dynamic **Form T/348M** payloads.
7. **Freight Dynamic Forecaster & Calm Window Finder (USP #2):**
   - Endpoint calculating hourly goods train density for specified corridor sections.
   - Algorithmic detector finding time gaps $\ge 2.5$ hours with zero coal/oil rake conflicts ("Calm Windows").
8. **Weather Risk Adaptation Engine (USP #3):**
   - Endpoint accepting weather alerts (rainfall intensity, soil moisture, rail temperature).
   - Dynamic re-scheduling hook: marks vulnerable sections as restricted and shifts outdoor maintenance to safe slots.
9. **Human-in-the-Loop Controller Actions:**
   - Endpoints for `approve`, `modify`, and `reject` block plans, updating possession status in real time.

---

### Phase 3: Polish & Refinement (Differentiators & Proof Points) — *Final Polish*

10. **Cross-Department Bidding System (USP #4):**
    - Endpoints for submitting, listing, and pooling maintenance bids.
11. **Explainable AI (XAI) & Regulatory Citations:**
    - Endpoint returning feature attribution breakdowns (TGI Urgency, Traffic Gap, OHE Interlock, Crew Proximity) and official regulatory manual citations (IRPWM Para 808, ACTM Vol II Para 2063, IRSEM Section 3).
12. **Tamper-Evident Audit Ledger:**
    - Persistent event ledger tracking every grant, cancellation, and Private Number generation.
